Settlo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Settlo is an app for splitting trip, flatmate and group expenses. It is available on Android, as a web app, and (where offered) on iOS. Settlo is developed and operated by Prasanna, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Settlo collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

In short

We collect only what Settlo needs to work: your account details and the groups and expenses you create. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it in plain text), username, and optionally your phone number and profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Preferences: your default currency and app settings.
  • Groups: group names, descriptions, currency, budgets, cover photos, and the list of members.
  • Expenses and payments: amounts, descriptions, categories, dates, who paid, how each expense is split, recurring expense schedules, settlements between members, and the history of edits to an expense.
  • Content you add to expenses: comments, reactions and receipt photos.
  • Information about other people: when you add someone to a group as a guest, you provide their name. When you invite someone, you provide their email address, username or phone number so we can find their Settlo account. Please only add people who are happy to be included.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, your device's push token, so we can send you alerts.
  • App version and platform (for example "1.7.1, Android" or "web"), so we can ask you to update when an old version stops working.
  • Technical data needed to run the service: like any online service, our providers (listed in section 4) process your IP address and basic request information when the app connects to them.

We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, SMS, browsing history or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run Settlo's features: groups, expense splitting, balances, settlements, budgets, recurring expenses, reports and summaries.
  • To let you find and invite other Settlo users, and let them find and invite you.
  • To send in-app and push notifications about activity in your groups, such as new expenses, payments, invitations and reminders.
  • To provide support when you contact us, fix problems with your account (for example, if you lose access to it), and keep Settlo secure and working.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for any purpose unrelated to Settlo.

3. What other people can see

  • Members of your groups can see your name, username, profile photo and email address, and everything in the groups you share: expenses, splits, balances, settlements, comments, reactions and receipt photos.
  • Other Settlo users can find your account only by entering your exact email address, username or phone number. They then see your name, username and profile photo, so they can invite you. Settlo has no public directory and no browsing of other users.
  • If you share a report or summary (for example, a PDF or a WhatsApp message), it goes wherever you send it. Reports are created on your device.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • Settlo's database runs on Google Cloud in Mumbai, India (asia-south1). Some Google and Expo services, such as sign-in and push delivery, may process data in other countries.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google.
  • Access is restricted by security rules: you can read only your own profile and the groups you belong to. Changes that affect other people, like membership and balances, are checked on our servers.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
  • Groups, expenses and settlements: until the group is deleted. These records are shared with the other members, so they stay while the group exists, even if you leave it or delete your account (see section 7).
  • Notifications: automatically deleted after about 60 days.
  • Backups: we may keep backup copies of the database to recover from failures. Deleted data is removed from backups as they are replaced, within 90 days.

7. Deleting your account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Settlo and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This works in the Android app and the web app.

By email

If you can't sign in, email settlo.help@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Settlo account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, username, phone number, profile photo and push tokens.
  • Your sign-in account.
  • Your notifications and invitations.
  • Any group where you are the only registered member, with all its expenses, settlements and photos.

What is kept

In groups shared with other people, expenses and settlements you were part of stay, so the other members' balances remain correct. In those groups your name is shown as "Your Name (deleted)", and your email and photo are removed. To remove these records too, ask the group's admin to delete the group, or contact us.

Deleting other data without deleting your account

You can delete expenses you created, groups you own, your profile photo, and receipt photos at any time in the app.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws elsewhere, you can:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update it. Most details can be edited in the app's Profile tab.
  • Erase it, as described in section 7.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account. You can turn off push notifications in your device settings.
  • Raise a grievance with us (section 12), and if you are not satisfied with our response, with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to.

To make a request, email us from the address on your account. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Settlo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are under 18, please use Settlo only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Device permissions

  • Notifications: to show alerts about your groups. Optional.
  • Photos and camera: only when you choose a profile photo, group cover or receipt. Only the photo you pick is uploaded; Settlo does not access the rest of your photo library.
  • Internet: to sync your data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Settlo changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app before they take effect.
