Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 14, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Settlo ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional group expense tracking experience, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile photo when you register via Firebase Authentication.
  • Trip & Expense Data: Group names, member names, expense amounts, currencies, description of expenses, and dates stored in Firebase Firestore to calculate split costs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate group creation, expense splitting, and settling debts.
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your group database.
  • To display your profile details to other members of the groups you join.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is securely stored and managed using Google Firebase Services (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). We implement standard security rules to prevent unauthorized access, but please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase to power the backend database, authentication, and cloud storage. Firebase governs its own data storage policies in compliance with global standards.

5. Children's Privacy

Settlo does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have accidentally collected such information, please contact us immediately to have it deleted.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
